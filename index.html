Welcome to Campus Life Hub

Objective

Our goal is to create a centralized web application that brings useful student-life and recreation information together in one place.
The application could include campus events, intramural sports, recreation facilities, weather, locations, and other useful information. We also want to use multiple APIs to provide updated information and make the experience more convenient for students.
The goal is not to replace Northeastern’s existing platforms, but to make it easier for students to find information and access the appropriate services from one central location.
